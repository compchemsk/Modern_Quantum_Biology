# Contributing

Thank you for helping map the field!

* **Suggest a paper** → use the form on the website (by email or GitHub), or [open a "Suggest a paper" issue](../../issues/new?template=suggest-paper.yml) with its DOI or arXiv id. The curator decides.
* **Fix an entry** → edit `data/papers.yaml` and open a pull request. The *Build & validate* check must pass. Please don't edit `README.md` between the `ATLAS` markers, `bibliography/` or `docs/data/`, `RESOURCES.md` or `CITATION.cff` by hand — they are generated.
* **Add a book, review or tool** → edit `config/resources.yaml` in a pull request.
* **Scope.** In: spin-based quantum effects in biological or bio-derived systems (protein/biomolecular qubits, radical pairs, magnetoreception, spin chemistry in proteins, nuclear-spin proposals), and the photochemistry of the platforms they are built on (LOV domains, flavoproteins, fluorescent proteins). Out: quantum computing *applied to* biology (drug discovery, protein folding on quantum hardware) unless it involves a biological quantum system.
* **No PDFs** — link by DOI only.
